# Retention Analysis Report

## Decision

Prioritize a two-track intervention: resolve recent failed payments immediately, and run a structured re-engagement campaign for active members with five or fewer visits in the last 61 days. Focus local management attention on **Sisli**, where observed churn is **31.8%**, and review the onboarding/value proposition for **Student**, the highest-churn plan in this sample.

## Evidence

The governed population contains 1,200 members, of whom 323 cancelled by 31 July 2026. Attendance bands show a strong association between low visit frequency and cancellation, while recent payment failure creates a directly addressable operational risk. The final queue contains 244 active members and USD 13,282.00 in monthly dues.

## Recommended actions

1. Contact Critical and High cases within two business days, separating billing resolution from motivation outreach.
2. Give Sisli a weekly retention review by plan and tenure.
3. Trigger onboarding check-ins during the first 90 days and monitor 3-, 6-, and 12-month cohort retention.
4. Measure outreach outcomes with a holdout or phased rollout before claiming causal impact.

## Caveats

This is synthetic portfolio data. Churn is an observed historical status; attendance and payment patterns are associations, not proof that an intervention will prevent cancellation. Recent cohorts are excluded from milestone denominators until they have sufficient observation time.
