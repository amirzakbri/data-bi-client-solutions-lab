# Dashboard Specification

Audience: retail performance manager. Coverage: 1 Jan 2025–31 Jul 2026.

## Page 1 — Time Performance
Hero cards: Revenue, Revenue YTD, Revenue Prior Year, YoY %, Rolling 12M.
Visuals: monthly Revenue vs Prior Year line chart; monthly MoM % columns; YTD comparison by year; month verification table.
Filters: Year, Quarter, Category, Channel, Region.

## Page 2 — Repair Evidence
Cards: defects diagnosed, measures repaired, checks passed. Tables: broken-to-correct mapping, monthly baseline, relationship register. Use warning red only for broken logic and teal for validated outputs.
