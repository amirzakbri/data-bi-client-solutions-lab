# Redesigned Dashboard Specification

Audience: retail commercial director. Decision: determine whether the portfolio is on plan, identify the cause of variance, and assign product follow-up. Coverage: 1 Aug 2025–31 Jul 2026.

## Page 1 — Executive Performance
- Header: dashboard title, reporting window, last refresh note.
- Hero cards: Revenue, Target Attainment, Gross Profit, Gross Margin, Orders. Each card uses one metric and one directly related comparison.
- Trend: monthly Revenue vs Revenue Target, single dollar axis, 12 observed periods.
- Driver view: sorted horizontal Revenue by Category.
- Regional view: Target Attainment by Region with 100% reference.
- Action table: product, revenue, margin, attainment, and text status; Critical first.
- Global slicers: Month, Region, Channel, Category.

## Page 2 — Redesign Evidence
- Side-by-side before and after images.
- Ten-row design audit with severity and remediation.
- KPI reconciliation table proving identical totals.
- Metric definitions and accessibility notes.

No pie charts, gauges, dual axes, decorative backgrounds, or color-only status signals.
