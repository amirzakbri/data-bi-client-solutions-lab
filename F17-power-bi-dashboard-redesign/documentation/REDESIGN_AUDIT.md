# Dashboard Redesign Audit

The source data and KPI definitions remain constant across the before and after views. The redesign changes information hierarchy, chart choice, period context, labeling, color semantics, and actionability—not the business result.

| Area | Before | After | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Every number competes for attention | Five hero KPIs, then movement, drivers, action | Executives see status before detail |
| Trend | Dual-axis revenue and margin | Revenue against target on one dollar axis | Removes scale ambiguity |
| Mix | Rainbow pie with many slices | Sorted horizontal category bars | Enables accurate comparison |
| Status | Color-only alerts | Text labels, ordering, and restrained tone | Accessible and interpretable |
| Filters | Dense slicer wall | Date, region, channel, category only | Reduces cognitive load |
| Follow-up | No exception view | Product action queue | Converts insight into work |
| Trust | No period or freshness label | Explicit Aug 2025–Jul 2026 window | Prevents mixed-period reading |

The complete ten-item audit is available in `solution/redesign_audit.csv`.
