# Website Lead Conversion Analysis — Decision Brief

## Recommendation
Protect a two-hour response SLA, concentrate performance campaigns on high-intent landing pages, and shift a test portion of paid budget toward the strongest observed ROAS source. Keep lead volume and lead-to-customer rate visible together so low-quality scale does not look successful.

## Governed result
- 1,800 unique leads, 133 customers, and 7.39% lead-to-customer conversion
- $1,384,385.03 won revenue and $769.10 revenue per lead
- 731 qualified leads and 367 opportunities
- Median first response: 5.1 hours
- 271 operational follow-up rows

## Action plan
1. Route Pricing and Consultation submissions to an expedited queue.
2. Set alerts for unanswered leads approaching two hours.
3. Run a controlled paid-budget reallocation for four weeks; compare qualified lead rate, CAC, and won revenue.
4. Review the opportunity and slow-response queue weekly with sales ownership assigned.

## Caveat
The dataset supports prioritization, not causal proof. Source mix, offer, geography, and response time are correlated; use experiments before a permanent budget shift.
