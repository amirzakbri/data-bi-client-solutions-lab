# Metric Dictionary

- **Lead:** one unique submitted website lead (`LeadID`).
- **Qualified lead:** a lead accepted against the business qualification rule.
- **Opportunity:** a qualified lead advanced to an active commercial opportunity.
- **Customer / Converted:** an opportunity marked won. This is the conversion numerator.
- **Lead-to-customer rate:** customers divided by all unique leads in the same segment.
- **Revenue per lead:** won revenue divided by all leads, including non-converters.
- **ROAS:** won revenue divided by recorded marketing spend. Organic and direct sources are not assigned an artificial ROAS.
- **First response time:** elapsed hours from lead submission to the first human response.

The reporting window is 1 January–30 June 2026. Revenue is attributed only to won leads and is not a lifetime-value estimate.
