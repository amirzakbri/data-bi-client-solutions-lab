# Social Media Monthly Performance Report

A client-ready reporting system for a small marketing agency that consolidates five social platforms into one governed monthly performance view.

## Business problem
Platform exports encouraged vanity-metric reporting and made it difficult to compare content quality, paid efficiency, follower growth, and conversion outcomes.

## Solution
The Excel report separates source data, calculations, targets, KPI definitions, content rankings, executive actions, and QA. July 2026 is compared with June and with editable targets.

## Deliverables
- Formula-driven Excel monthly report
- 320-row realistic synthetic dataset
- Platform and content output tables
- KPI dictionary and editable targets
- 12-control validation pack
- Executive preview and management report

## Tools and skills
Excel, marketing analytics, KPI governance, data validation, performance reporting, content analysis, paid-media efficiency.

## Reuse
Replace or append platform-export rows in **Source Data**, retain the governed column structure, and refresh the month/platform summary formulas.

## Service connection
Suitable for monthly social-media reporting, campaign review, content optimization, and paid-budget allocation engagements.
