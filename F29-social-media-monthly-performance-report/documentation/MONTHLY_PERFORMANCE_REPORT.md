# Monthly Performance Report — July 2026

## Executive summary
- July delivered **1,053,266 reach**, **6.04% engagement**, and **1,742 tracked conversions**.
- Attributed revenue reached **$230,678** on **$42,115** spend, a **5.48x ROAS**.
- The strongest balanced content was **SM0303** (YouTube, Short Video); it should be repurposed and used as a creative pattern.
- Paid budget should shift toward platforms above the 3.5x target; weak creatives should be paused before additional spend.

## Measurement rules
Engagement rate uses reach as the denominator. CTR uses impressions. Conversion rate uses clicks. CPC and ROAS use paid spend; organic posts remain visible but are not interpreted as zero-cost paid efficiency. Revenue is attributed only to tracked conversions.

## Recommended actions
1. Scale the top five content patterns across adjacent platforms.
2. Refresh or pause paid posts below target ROAS.
3. Review community-response exceptions above 24 hours.
4. Reuse the same governed template next month and compare against both July and targets.

## Caveat
This portfolio project uses deterministic synthetic data. Platform-reported reach is not deduplicated across platforms, so the cross-platform reach total is a summed platform measure, not unique people.
