# F30 — Small-Business 13-Week Cash-Flow Forecast

## Client problem
A growing service business needed to understand whether its bank balance could cover payroll, suppliers, rent, taxes, operating costs, and planned equipment purchases while customer receipts arrived unevenly.

## Solution delivered
A rolling, formula-driven 13-week cash forecast with centralized assumptions, scenario selection, weekly liquidity alerts, automatic facility draws, an executive dashboard, scenario comparison, and six audit controls.

## Business value
- Identifies the precise weeks when cash falls below the minimum reserve
- Quantifies peak short-term funding need
- Compares Base, Upside, and Downside collection/payment conditions
- Gives management an actionable weekly refresh process
- Separates editable inputs from calculations and keeps an auditable cash roll-forward
