# Model guide

1. Update opening cash, minimum reserve, credit facility, and scenario assumptions.
2. Replace the yellow weekly input schedule with current receipts and committed payments.
3. Select Base, Upside, or Downside in Assumptions!B5.
4. Review the Executive Summary, funding requirement, and weekly headroom.
5. Confirm every row on the Checks sheet remains PASS.

Sign convention: receipts are positive; payments are entered as positive uses of cash and subtracted by the model. Currency is USD.
